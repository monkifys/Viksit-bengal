📋 About This Section

The WB Accountability Tracker focuses on government promises and administrative actions. But some of the most important journalism about West Bengal — ground reports, investigative pieces, video dispatches from the field — has no place in a promise tracker, and yet deserves to be read.

Press Watch is a curated editorial layer: a small selection of journalistic pieces we think deserve wider attention. These are not government updates. They are not evidence for or against any promise. They are simply journalism worth reading or watching.

What qualifies: Ground reports, video investigations, long-form journalism, and data-driven analyses about West Bengal from credible publications and independent journalists. We add items sparingly — only when we believe a piece offers genuine insight into the ground reality.
