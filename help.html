📋 3-Step Civic Resolution Pathway
Administrative Escalation
Step 1 — Local Assistance

Bangla Sahayata Kendra (BSK)

Visit your local Gram Panchayat or BDO office BSK centre. Over 3,500+ centres across West Bengal provide 100% free offline assistance for all state scheme applications.

Step 2 — Formal Grievance

Official Grievance Portals

If an application is stalled, submit a formal written complaint with your reference number on prdgrievance.wb.gov.in (State) or pgportal.gov.in (Central).

Step 3 — Legal Escalation

Right to Information (RTI)

If an official decision is delayed without written cause, file an online RTI on rtionline.wb.gov.in with a ₹10 fee to obtain official file movement records.
