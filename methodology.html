Methodology & Evidence Standards

Our complete operational framework for non-partisan factual verification, 11-stage status transitions, and primary source grading.

1. Foundational Civic Principles

The Viksit Bengal Dashboard operates as an evidence-based civic development platform rather than an unofficial government portal or partisan campaign site.

  • Commitment vs. Outcome Distinction: The platform rigorously separates a political promise, an administrative announcement, a budgetary sanction, physical construction, and measured civic outcomes.
  • No Attributed Causality Without Evidence: The dashboard never claims that a specific party or government caused an economic or social outcome unless direct, audited documentary evidence supports it.
  • Zero Invented Data: If baseline statistics or progress documentation are unavailable, the platform displays "Awaiting Verification" or "Data Unavailable" rather than fabricating figures.

2. Standardized 11-Stage Status Model

Every public commitment and infrastructure project is categorized under an explicit 11-state transition lifecycle:

Status Code Standard Label Verification Criteria
not_started Not Started (○) Logged verbatim commitment with no administrative or legal action initiated.
announced Announced (📢) Public ministerial or cabinet announcement recorded in press conference or official briefing.
legally_initiated Legally Initiated (⚖️) Cabinet note approved, bill introduced in Assembly, or formal draft published.
funded Funded (💰) Budgetary line item sanctioned or external institutional loan agreement cleared.
under_implementation Under Implementation (◑) Active tenders floated, physical civil works commenced, or rollout operationalized.
partially_delivered Partially Delivered (⛅) Substantial project milestones operational or initial beneficiary tranches distributed.
delivered Delivered (✓) Defined measurable completion criteria fulfilled with Grade A or B documentary evidence.
delayed Delayed (⏳) Project or commitment exceeded announced milestone schedule without operational status.
reversed Reversed (↩️) Action structurally dismantled, statutory repeal enacted, or scheme officially abandoned.
disputed Disputed (⚠️) Active judicial stay, conflicting audit findings, or contradictory institutional claims.
not_verifiable Not Verifiable (❓) Absence of transparent public documentation or unquantifiable commitment definition.

3. Evidence Confidence Grading (A–D)

Every update and candidate news item is tagged with an evidence hierarchy grade:

Grade A — Primary Official Evidence

Official West Bengal Government Gazette notifications, enacted Assembly Acts, Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) audit releases, MOSPI/RBI statistical bulletins, and official state budget documents.

Grade B — Credible Secondary Reporting

National news wires (PTI, ANI) and investigative reporting from established publications (The Hindu, Indian Express, Business Standard, The Telegraph, Times of India).

Grade C — Public & Institutional Claims

Press statements, political briefings, or organizational claims that have not yet been independently verified by primary documentation.

Grade D — Unverified Material

Unattributed social media claims or anecdotal assertions. Never used as sole justification for changing a commitment or project status.

4. Autonomous Scraper & AI Safety Boundaries

The autonomous crawler (scraper.js) ingests published RSS feeds and categorizes updates, but adheres to strict governance:

  • No Autonomous Publication: AI models never unilaterally change statuses or publish updates live. All candidate items stage in an Influx Review Queue for authorized human reviewer verification.
  • Strict Neutrality: AI assists in keyword matching and language translation, but is prohibited from making editorial assertions about criminal guilt, corruption, or contested political disputes.
